Occupancy sensor

Your phone at a door, counting who goes in and out.

  1. Place your phone at the side of a door, camera facing across the doorway, so people walk through the picture from one side to the other. Lean it against something; it must not move.
  2. Press start and step out of the picture. After three seconds the app takes a picture of the empty door and compares every following frame with it.
  3. Someone walking from the left half to the right half counts as in, the other way as out. If it is the wrong way round for your door, swap it later.

Nothing leaves your phone. The camera picture is only compared in this page, no images are stored or sent anywhere. The event list stays in this browser until you download it or press reset.
